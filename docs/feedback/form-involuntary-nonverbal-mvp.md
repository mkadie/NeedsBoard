# Feedback Form — Involuntary Non-Verbal (MVP)

Variant id: `involuntary-nonverbal-mvp`. Built from
[`FORM_TEMPLATE.md`](./FORM_TEMPLATE.md). This is the **primary feedback form on
tssfaa.com** for the MVP, backed by a Google Sheet that feeds the
`feedback-integrate` workflow.

## Intro text

> We're building this device *with* you, not just for you. There are no wrong
> answers, nothing is required, and you can stay completely anonymous. Tell us as
> much or as little as you like — even one sentence helps the people who come
> after you.

## Shared questions

1. **Which best describes you?** *(context)*
   - I experience involuntary non-verbal moments myself
   - Family member / friend
   - Teacher / aide
   - Speech, occupational, or mental-health professional
   - Other
2. **When your words won't come, what do you most need to be able to say?**
   *(product / wording)* — examples welcome: *"I need a minute," "Can you write it
   down?", "I'm OK."*
3. **The emergency message currently says:** *"Please stand back. I cannot speak
   right now, but I am OK. Crowding me makes it worse. Thank you."* **Does this
   wording work for you? What would you change?** *(wording)*
4. **What would make a device feel safe to pull out in public — and what would
   make it worse?** *(product)*
5. **Is anything in the documentation wrong, confusing, or not true to your
   experience?** *(wording)*
6. **What's missing? What do you wish it did?** *(product)*
7. **Anything else you want us to know?** *(context)*
8. **Optional — stay in touch.** *(context)* — mailing list / join test group / email.

## Variant-specific block

9. **How would you most want to trigger it?** *(config / product — multi-choice +
   free text)*
   - A dial you turn and press (the default)
   - A touch screen
   - One big button
   - Something else: ____
10. **It's meant to live in a pocket and be used now and then, not all day. Does
    that match how you'd use it?** *(product / config)*
